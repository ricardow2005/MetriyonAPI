# Code of Conduct

MetriyonAPI contributors are expected to keep project discussions professional, constructive and focused on the software.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory behavior, deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, close or remove contributions and discussions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

For conduct concerns that should not be handled publicly, use the contact channels at https://www.organizza.com.br and identify the message as related to the MetriyonAPI open-source project.
